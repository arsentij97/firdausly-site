Questions

When does Firdausly launch?

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The “Coming soon to the App Store” button already leads to the app's page.

Will there be an Android version?

iPhone first. Android is planned; we'll announce it on Instagram @firdaus.social.

Can everyone see my prayers?

No. Your check-ins are visible only to the people you added yourself.

What if my family isn't on Firdausly yet?

Send them an invite link. The reminder card and the invite to pray together open without a subscription, and with your code they get 7 more days of Plus on top of the 14-day trial.

How are prayer times calculated?

In Kazakhstan, by the official DUMK table; in Tatarstan, by DUM RT; in Turkey, by Diyanet. Everywhere else, by the calculation method and madhab you choose.

Will Focus really lock Instagram?

Yes, through Apple's Screen Time. Only you choose what to lock, in Apple's own window. Check off your prayer, and the apps unlock. There's always an emergency exit.

Is there music in Reminders?

No. Voice, words and nasheeds without instruments. Before it's shown, every video is checked: frames, speech and text.

How much does it cost?

The essentials are free forever. Firdausly Plus is $9.99 a month or $59.99 a year in the US, and the first 14 days are free. Prices for your region are shown in the App Store before you pay. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period.
