Terms with no fine print

What you get

  • 20% of every payment and renewal by people who came with your code: monthly or yearly, Plus or Premium. As long as they pay, you get your share. One level only: we don't pay for creators you bring in, and you never pay to take part.

  • What we ask in return: speak about prayer with care, without shaming anyone for missed prayers and without promising rewards from the Almighty; show only what's already in the app; label ads the way the law in your country requires.

Questions

How many followers do I need?

There's no minimum. What matters is that people trust you and that your audience prays. A hundred people who listen matter more than a hundred thousand passers-by.

How is the 20% calculated?

From the net revenue of each payment: VAT, Apple's commission and a 4% tax come off the price. For a month of Firdausly Plus in Kazakhstan (3,790 ₸), you get 533 ₸. The same goes for every renewal, for as long as the person pays.

Who pays me?

Payouts are made by the sole proprietor who owns Firdausly, under a standard partner agreement. We'll send you the agreement together with your code.

Is there a second level?

No. We pay only on payments from people who came with your code. We don't pay for bringing in other creators.

How are payouts made if I'm not in Kazakhstan?

Once a month, by bank transfer to your country; in Kazakhstan, to Kaspi. The minimum is 10,000 ₸, about $20 in your local currency. We'll agree on the transfer details when you join.

What does my audience get?

30 days of Firdausly Plus with your code. The code is entered within the first 7 days after signing up in the app.

When can I start talking about it?

Your code goes live when Firdausly launches on the App Store. Apply early so your code is ready on day one.

Do I have to be exclusive?

No. Instagram, TikTok and YouTube stay yours. Talk about Firdausly wherever people listen to you.
